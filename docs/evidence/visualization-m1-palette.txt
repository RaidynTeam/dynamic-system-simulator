Reading of the results:
- On the light card the agreed identities pass every check that applies.
- On the dark card Exact #475569 reaches 1.93:1 and RK4 #2563EB 2.83:1,
  both under the 3:1 floor for non-text marks. This is the measurement
  behind the dark-mode proposal in docs/modules/visualization.md.
- The shipped dark variant clears 3:1 for all three and raises the worst
  adjacent CVD separation from 8.4 to 16.7.
- The chroma-floor FAIL on the reference curve is deliberate: Exact is
  intentionally neutral grey, and line style carries the identity so the
  distinction never depends on colour alone.
- The dark lightness-band FAIL is the generic band for a mid-tone surface;
  the dark card needs lighter steps than the band allows.
