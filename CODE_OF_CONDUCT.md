# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Dynamic System Simulator to be respectful, welcoming,
and focused on learning and building a reliable educational tool.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions, reviews, and reports.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give teammates room to learn, ask questions, and disagree in good faith.
- Keep project spaces free from harassment, discrimination, and personal
  attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without
permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy privately to the repository
administrators through GitHub. They will review reports, protect the privacy of
the people involved, and take any action appropriate to the situation, including
removing content or restricting participation. Contact a RaidynTeam organization
owner through GitHub if the repository's private reporting option is unavailable.

This policy applies to project spaces and when someone represents the project
or its community.
