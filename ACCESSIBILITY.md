# Accessibility

Dynamic System Simulator is a PySide6 desktop application. We aim to keep its
controls and plots usable by people with different access needs.

When changing the interface:

- Make every action available from the keyboard and keep focus visible.
- Give controls clear labels and accessible names; do not rely on icon-only
  meaning.
- Keep text readable and maintain strong contrast in both light and dark themes.
- Do not use color as the only way to distinguish methods or communicate state.
- Keep status and error messages available to assistive technology.
- Check that layouts remain usable at the supported window sizes and with larger
  text where the platform allows it.

Please report an accessibility barrier in a GitHub issue. Include the relevant
screen or control, the steps that led to the barrier, and the platform or assistive
technology involved when you can.
